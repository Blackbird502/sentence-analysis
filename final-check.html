Final Check: Sentence Analysis

What Is That Word Doing? · John Archambault · ChalkLine

Analyze six sentences you haven't seen

Build each analysis yourself, working down the questions in order, the same way every lesson did. The diagram draws itself as you answer.

  • Tap words on the tape to answer the first questions: what's going on, who or what, whom or what, to whom or for whom, and any more ands.
  • Then go back to the beginning. For each remaining word or phrase, choose what it's doing and which word it hangs from.
  • A sentence counts as correct if you finish it with no more than one slip. If a choice is wrong, you'll get a hint and can try again.
  • To pass, analyze at least five of the six sentences correctly. Each new attempt draws a different set.
